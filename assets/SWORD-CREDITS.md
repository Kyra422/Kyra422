## Changes

The supplied mesh geometry is retained. The rendered adaptation changes the guard and grip to teal, adds warm gold material accents, adjusts the silver blade material, and adds studio lighting, a camera, an upright charging motion, a sword swing and a light-wave effect. The profile banner combines the rendered sword with a Skyward Sword-inspired sky illustration and profile text.
